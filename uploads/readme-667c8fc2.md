Design a high-fidelity homepage mockup for the institutional website of the
Inspection Générale des Finances (IGF) of the Democratic Republic of Congo —
a government anti-corruption / public-finance oversight body. Desktop first
(1440px), and show a mobile variant. All UI copy in French.

CONTEXT & TONE
Sober, institutional, trustworthy, modern. This is an official state website,
not a startup. Clean, generous whitespace, strong hierarchy, fully accessible
(WCAG 2.1 AA). The institution is modernizing under a 2026–2028 strategic plan,
so it should feel current but dignified.

BRAND SYSTEM (mandatory — from the RDC government charte graphique)
- Primary institutional blue: #0095C9 (Congo flag blue) — headers, links, key UI
- Tricolor accents (use sparingly as a recurring thin state-line motif, in this
  order): blue #0095C9 → yellow #FFED00 → red #DB3832
- Deep navy (a dark shade of the institutional blue) for the footer and headings
- White + light grey (#F5F7FA) section backgrounds; dark text (#1A1A2E)
- Typography: Cooper Hewitt for all headings, nav and UI (Semibold for the
  official institution name, Book/Medium for body). EB Garamond reserved for the
  "Mot du Chef de Service" quote and any formal citation. Great Vibes only for the
  honorific line "L'Inspecteur Général des Finances – Chef de Service."
- Logo: lockup of the RDC coat of arms (armoiries) + "INSPECTION GÉNÉRALE DES
  FINANCES" (use a placeholder crest if no asset). On dark backgrounds the logo
  goes monochrome white.

HEADER (sticky)
- Thin top utility strip: "RÉPUBLIQUE DÉMOCRATIQUE DU CONGO" + tricolor bar
- Main bar: logo + institution name on the left; primary nav on the right with
  the active item highlighted in blue; search icon at the far right.
- Nav items (this exact arborescence, from the cahier des charges):
  Accueil · L'IGF-RDC · Actualités & Médias · Publications & Ressources ·
  Cartographie · Signalement · Événements · Contact
- Mobile: hamburger menu.

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS (in order)
1. Hero carousel — full-width institutional photos, a strong French headline, a
   short subtitle, one primary CTA. Slide indicators.
2. Accès rapides — 4 service tiles with icons: "Signaler / Dénoncer",
   "Demander une visite", "Consulter la cartographie", "Rapports & publications".
3. Actualités — grid of 4 cards (category tag, date, title, thumbnail) + "Voir
   tout" link.
4. Mot du Chef de Service — portrait + a pull-quote set in EB Garamond, signed.
5. Cartographie interactive (teaser) — a stylized map of the DRC with filter
   chips for the entités assujetties au contrôle de l'IGF, and a CTA to the full
   map.
6. Publications & Ressources — list of latest audit reports / studies with a PDF
   download button each.
7. Médiathèque — row of video thumbnails with play overlays.
8. Événements à venir — 2–3 upcoming events with date blocks and an "S'inscrire" CTA.
9. Réseaux sociaux — a row of social cards (Facebook, YouTube, X/Twitter, LinkedIn).

FOOTER (deep navy, with the tricolor state-line accent)
- Logo + institution name; real contact details:
  30, Avenue des Forces Armées, Kinshasa/Gombe · BP 3683 ·
  +243 97 105 96 54 · contact@igf.gouv.cd · www.igf.gouv.cd
- Columns: "Accès rapide" (site sections) and "Liens utiles" (Présidence,
  Gouvernement, Ministères, Documentation).
- Bottom: copyright + mentions légales, with a small tricolor mark.

Make it feel like a credible national institution. Prioritise clarity, contrast
and a confident, restrained use of the flag colors.
